Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account with Spendly, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use the app, we store information about your expenses including category, amount, date, and description. We also collect basic usage data such as login times and device information to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal and financial data is used solely to provide you with the expense tracking service. We use this information to display your expense summaries, generate reports, and help you analyze your spending patterns. We do not use your data for marketing, analytics, or any purpose other than providing our service. We never sell or share your personal financial data with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

All of your data is stored securely on our servers with encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of your data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not use third-party services to process your financial data. We do not integrate with any external analytics, advertising, or data processing platforms. Your data remains entirely within our system. If we ever integrate third-party services in the future, we will update this policy and obtain your explicit consent.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete any individual expense at any time, or you can delete your entire account along with all associated data. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. We do not retain backup copies of deleted personal data for longer than 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time. If you have questions about how we handle your data or wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We are committed to addressing your privacy concerns promptly and transparently.
